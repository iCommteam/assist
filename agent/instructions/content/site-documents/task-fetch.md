- A site gets files back only through the browser worker's `save_file`, which keeps a PDF for the site to download; only a task pinned to a saved login has it. For an invoice job, a task that asks for the invoices or receipts of a period, assign the worker all of it: sign in, open the account's billing or invoices page, and for every invoice or receipt dated in the requested period save its PDF with `save_file`, preferring the supplier's own Download PDF link and opening Stripe's hosted invoice page when that is where the link goes. Tell it to never pay, buy, upgrade, cancel, submit or change anything beyond the sign-in and the search, filter, or download that finds the documents. Then end the task with `done` when the worker saved files, or `nothing_found` when the period has no invoice or receipt. An invoice job without a pinned login ends with `blocked`. Name the saved files in your reply, never their download addresses. Whether the task brought anything back is decided by what it actually saved, never by the result or the message, so never claim work that did not happen.
