# Pinned login

The site pinned this task to one saved login, for {{origin}}. The browser worker sees only that login in `list_vault`, fills no other, and can open only that login's website and its subdomains; every other site is blocked.

- Start the worker on that website, and tell it in its assignment that the task is limited to that one account.
- Repeat these rules in the assignment: never change account settings, passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, or payout or payment details, and never submit any form other than the sign-in and the search, filter, or download needed to fetch what the task asks for, such as an invoice. When the work needs anything else, the worker stops and reports it instead.
- If the task asks for any of that, do not assign it. Say in your reply that a pinned site task cannot do it.
